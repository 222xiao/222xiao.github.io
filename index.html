About

I am Dongfu Xiao, an undergraduate student in Computer Science at the City University of Macau.

My research focuses on evaluating large language models for computer science education and on privacy-preserving computer vision. I am interested in how these systems perform on real tasks and where their limitations matter.

Advisor: Assistant Professor Chi Liu

Research Interests

  • Large language models for computer science education
  • Cross-lingual and domain-specific model evaluation
  • Privacy-preserving computer vision and medical imaging
