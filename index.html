About me

I am an undergraduate student at the City University of Macau. My research explores how artificial intelligence can support computer science education and how AI systems can be evaluated and used responsibly.

My recent work includes cross-lingual evaluation of large language models, an empirical study of DeepSeek in computer education, and privacy protection for retinal images. I am advised by Assistant Professor Chi Liu.
